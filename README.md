## Introduction

In this document, we analyze three investment options—Apple (AAPL), Intel Corporation (INTL), and Kroger (KR). We present various metrics such as the arithmetic mean, geometric mean, standard deviation, coefficient of variation (CV), beta, and the coefficient of determination (𝑅𝑅²). These variables are calculated from historical stock data to assist in evaluating the potential risks and returns associated with each stock.

## Investment Options

The stocks analyzed are:

- **Apple Inc. (AAPL)**: A major player in the information technology sector.
- **Intel Corporation (INTL)**: A key player in the semiconductor industry.
- **Kroger Co. (KR)**: A leading supermarket chain in the United States.

## Financial Metrics

The analysis includes the following key financial metrics:

- **Arithmetic Mean**: The average monthly return for each stock.
- **Geometric Mean**: A measure of the average rate of return, taking into account compounding.
- **Standard Deviation**: A measure of the volatility or risk associated with each stock’s return.
- **Coefficient of Variation (CV)**: The ratio of risk to expected return.
- **Beta**: The relationship between the stock's return and the overall market (S&P 500).
- **Coefficient of Determination (𝑅𝑅²)**: The proportion of the stock’s performance explained by the S&P 500.

## Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)

The Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) is used to calculate the expected rate of return for each stock given its level of risk. The analysis compares the beta values of each stock to the S&P 500 and evaluates their correlation with the market.

## Investment Recommendation

Based on the analysis, we recommend the following:

- **Apple (AAPL)**: Apple is the most resilient stock, providing the highest returns with the lowest risk relative to its expected return. It has a positive relationship with the S&P 500, making it a strong investment option.
- **Intel (INTL)**: While Intel offers solid returns, it exhibits more volatility and a lower correlation with the S&P 500.
- **Kroger (KR)**: Kroger is the least recommended option due to its high risk and low return compared to the other two stocks.

## Risk Factors

There are two primary types of risks that affect investments:

1. **Market Risk**: This is inherent to the overall market, such as economic shocks (e.g., the COVID-19 pandemic).
2. **Company-Specific Risk**: This includes risks related to company-specific events, such as leadership changes or shifts in consumer sentiment.

## Conclusion

Apple is the most recommended stock for investment, with its robust financial performance and strong future prospects. Intel, while a good option, is more volatile and less correlated with the broader market. Kroger, due to its higher risk and lower return, is the least favorable investment.
